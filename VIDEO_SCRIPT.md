## 📋 Production Blueprint & Pre-Recording Checklist

### 1. Recording Setup & Environment

- [ ] **Browser**: Chrome/Brave in Fullscreen (`F11`), 100% zoom, bookmarks bar hidden, dev notifications disabled.
- [ ] **Wallet**: Freighter Wallet installed on Stellar Testnet, funded with at least 10,000 test XLM via Friendbot.
- [ ] **State**: A freshly prepared canvas state with some existing pixel art and live leaderboard entries.
- [ ] **Secondary Wallet / Event Simulator**: Optional second browser window or background script ready to trigger a paint transaction to demonstrate real-time live event streaming.
- [ ] **Audio & Music**:
  - Voiceover: Clean, warm, energetic, and articulate.
  - Background Music: Subtle, upbeat electronic/cyberpunk ambient lo-fi (ducked at -22dB during voiceover).
  - Sound Effects (SFX): Subtle UI whoosh, wallet chime, success bell, mouse click pops.

## 🎙️ Complete Voiceover Script (Single Reader Flow)

_(Estimated Word Count: 480 words | Reading Pace: ~130 words/minute | Total Time: ~3m 40s)_

> _"What happens when you bring collaborative digital art directly onto the blockchain? Welcome to **StellarCanvas** — a decentralized, 64-by-64 collaborative pixel canvas built natively on Stellar Soroban._
>
> _Every single pixel placed isn't just stored in a web database — it's an immutable, verifiable smart contract invocation. No centralized backend servers, no hidden caches — pure on-chain state with lightning-fast finality._
>
> _Under the hood, StellarCanvas is powered by three specialized Rust smart contracts: the Pixel Contract for canvas storage and row slicing, the Leaderboard Contract for live ranking, and the Achievement Contract for NFT badges. Let's dive right in and see it in action._
>
> _Connecting to Web3 on Stellar is instantaneous. With integrated StellarWalletsKit support, users can choose Freighter, Albedo, xBull, or Lobstr. Once connected, your wallet balance and public key are verified directly on the Stellar testnet, unlocking full interactive control of the canvas._
>
> _Here is the battlefield: a 4,096-pixel grid rendered on a high-performance HTML5 Canvas element. With full hardware acceleration, you get fluid multi-touch zoom up to 20x, smooth click-and-drag panning, and precise coordinate hover tracking. Selecting your weapon is simple: pick from 24 curated preset hex palettes or dial in any custom 24-bit RGB color with real-time visual previews._
>
> _Now, let's claim a pixel at coordinate 32, 32. Clicking the grid immediately initiates an on-chain `paint_pixel` invocation on our Soroban smart contract. Freighter prompts us to review and sign the transaction. We hit approve... and just like that — in less than two seconds, Stellar achieves deterministic finality! The pixel is permanently recorded on-chain, and an explorer link is instantly generated for complete transparency._
>
> _Notice how the rest of the application reacted. StellarCanvas features a decentralized Event Provider that continuously streams `PixelPainted` events directly from Soroban RPC endpoints. When other artists around the globe paint on the canvas, your screen updates in real time. It's a living, breathing collaborative digital canvas powered 100% by decentralized events._
>
> _Painting pixels isn't just about art — it's a competitive game. Hitting key milestones unlocks on-chain NFT-style achievement badges. In your Profile, you can inspect your earned badges, track progress toward higher tiers like Pixel Artist and Pixel Master, and review your historical paint logs with timestamped coordinates._
>
> _For the competitive painters, the Leaderboard provides a real-time hall of fame. Ranks 1 through 10 are queried directly from the Leaderboard smart contract, tracking the most prolific painters across the network. Scores update dynamically as events stream in, ensuring the leaderboard stays competitive every second of the day._
>
> _To respect Soroban's 100-entry persistent storage read budget, StellarCanvas implements row-sliced reads to seamlessly assemble the full 4,096 pixels. With 100% test coverage across all 18 contract tests and a rigorous CI/CD pipeline, it's production-ready._
>
> _StellarCanvas showcases the true potential of Stellar Soroban: high-throughput, low-cost, real-time decentralized applications. Connect your wallet, claim your coordinate, and make your mark on history. Check out the GitHub repo and try the live demo today!"_

## 🎥 Recording & Post-Production Tips

1. **Mouse Movement Smoothing**: Use a mouse smoothing utility or practice slow, deliberate cursor movements to keep viewer attention focused.
2. **Dynamic Zooming**: In post-production (Premiere, Final Cut, DaVinci Resolve, or ScreenFlow), add smooth 115%–130% punch-in zooms whenever focusing on the status banner, color picker, or wallet modal.
3. **Sound Design**: Add subtle sound effects on every UI interaction (click, pop on color select, whoosh on navigation) to give the video high production value.
4. **Captions**: Burn in stylish subtitles with high contrast font (Inter or Montserrat Semi-Bold, white with subtle dark drop shadow) for social media accessibility.
